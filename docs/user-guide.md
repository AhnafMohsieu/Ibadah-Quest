## Getting Started

1. **Open the app** — Navigate to `index.html` in a browser, or install as a PWA from your browser's menu.
2. **First-time setup** — The onboarding overlay will guide you through:
   - Setting your name
   - Choosing your starting title
   - Understanding the XP and streak system
3. **Start logging** — Tap any prayer or deed to begin earning XP immediately.

### PWA Installation

For the best experience, install Ibadah Quest as an app:

- **Android**: Tap "Add to Home Screen" in Chrome's menu
- **iOS**: Tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen"
- **Desktop**: Click the install icon in the address bar

Once installed, the app works offline and provides home-screen widgets.

## Daily Routine

A typical day with Ibadah Quest:

1. **Morning** — Open the app, log your Fajr prayer, read the Morning Adhkar
2. **Throughout the day** — Log prayers on time, complete extra deeds, check your quests
3. **Evening** — Log Evening Adhkar, track your Quran reading, give charity
4. **Night** — Log Isha, review your day, check achievements

Each action earns XP toward your next level. Maintain your streak by logging at least one prayer every day.

## Features

### Prayer Tracking

Tap to log each of the five daily prayers:

| Prayer | Time | XP |
|--------|------|----|
| Fajr | Dawn | 70 |
| Dhuhr | Noon | 50 |
| Asr | Afternoon | 40 |
| Maghrib | Sunset | 50 |
| Isha | Night | 60 |

**Jummah (Friday)** replaces Dhuhr with 100 XP.

**Perfect Day Bonus**: Log all 5 prayers to earn a "Perfect Day" badge and bonus XP.

**Prayer Times Tab**: View countdown to the next prayer and full daily schedule.

### Quest System

Quests are challenges that earn bonus XP. There are four tiers:

#### Daily Quests
Refresh every day. Examples:
- Complete Fajr & Isha (55 XP)
- Do 3 extra deeds (50 XP)
- Read Quran today (35 XP)
- Pray Tahajjud (70 XP)

#### Weekly Quests
Reset every Sunday. Examples:
- Perfect prayers 5 days (400 XP)
- Read Surah Kahf on Friday (250 XP)
- Maintain a 7-day streak (300 XP)

#### Monthly Quests
Reset on the 1st. Examples:
- 20 perfect days this month (1200 XP)
- Read Quran 15 times (1000 XP)
- Fast 3 days (800 XP)

#### Yearly Quests
Long-term challenges:
- 300 perfect days this year (8000 XP)
- Read Quran 300 times (10,000 XP)
- Memorize 5 new surahs (12,000 XP)

| Tier | Color | Examples |

| Bronze | Copper | First Step, Perfect Day, Quran Reader |
| Silver | Silver | 7-Day Streak, 100 Prayers, Quest Master |
| Gold | Gold | 30-Day Streak, 500 Prayers, Level 25 |
| Platinum | Platinum | 100-Day Streak, 1000 Prayers |

Achievements unlock automatically as you complete deeds, maintain streaks, and reach milestones.

### Level Progression

Earn XP to level up through 300+ unique titles:

- **Level 1**: New Seeker
- **Level 10**: Soft Heart
- **Level 25**: Reflective Heart
- **Level 50**: Mountain of Trust
- **Level 100**: Tahlil of Oneness
- **Level 150+**: Advanced spiritual titles

Each level requires more XP than the last. Your level and title are displayed in the top bar.

### Consistency Bonuses

The app rewards you for returning regularly:

- **Comeback Bonus**: +50 XP for returning the next day
- **Welcome Back**: +100 XP after 2+ days away
- **Weekly Consistency**:
  - 5 perfect days: +100 XP
  - 6 perfect days: +200 XP
  - 7 perfect days (Perfect Week): +500 XP

### Combo Streaks

| Streak | XP Multiplier |

Combos display in a grid on the home screen showing current count, multiplier, and best streak.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the app work offline?**
A: Yes. All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. No internet connection required after first load.

**Q: How do I back up my data?**
A: Your data is stored in localStorage. To back up, you can export it manually or use browser developer tools. A future update may add export functionality.

**Q: Can I use the app on multiple devices?**
A: Currently, data is stored locally per device. Each device maintains its own separate progress.

**Q: What happens if I miss a day?**
A: Your streak resets, but you receive a "Welcome Back" bonus (+100 XP) when you return. Use a Streak Freeze from the shop to protect your streak.

**Q: How do seasonal events work?**
A: Seasonal events (Ramadan, Hajj) activate automatically during the appropriate Islamic months. They provide bonus XP multipliers and special content.

**Q: Can I customize the app?**
A: Yes. You can:
- Toggle light/dark theme
- Choose your title
- Set personal goals
- Feature achievements
- Customize your profile

**Q: How do I earn more XP?**
A: 
- Log prayers on time
- Complete extra deeds (Quran, dhikr, charity)
- Finish quests (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Maintain streaks for multipliers
- Participate in seasonal events
- Complete journeys

**Q: What are the XP multipliers?**
A:
- Combo streaks: 1.5x to 5x
- Seasonal events: 1.5x (Hajj) or 2x (Ramadan)
- XP Boosts from shop: 2x for 24 hours

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. All data stays on your device in localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server.

**Q: How do I install the app?**
A: Use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App" option. The app works as a PWA with offline support.

**Q: Can I track multiple types of worship?**
A: Yes. The app tracks:
- 5 daily prayers
- 20+ voluntary prayers
- 100+ extra deeds
- Quran reading
- Dhikr
- Fasting
- Charity
- Health metrics
- Finance
- Mood
- And much more

**Q: How do achievements work?**
A: Achievements unlock automatically when you meet their requirements. They're organized by rarity from Bronze to Jannah.

**Q: What's the difference between streaks and combos?**
A: Streaks track consecutive days of overall activity. Combos track consecutive days of specific deed categories (e.g., Quran reading, charity).

**Q: How do I access the analytics dashboard?**
A: Go to Profile → Analytics. The dashboard loads charts and insights about your worship patterns.

**Q: Can I set reminders?**
A: The app includes a notification system. Enable notifications in your browser to receive prayer reminders.
