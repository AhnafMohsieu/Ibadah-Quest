## 2. Official Platform Guidance (2025–2026) & Multi-Script Research

### 2.1 Apple Human Interface Guidelines: Liquid Glass & RTL (`2025–2026`)
- **Liquid Glass Overview** (`https://developer.apple.com/documentation/TechnologyOverviews/liquid-glass`): Apple’s dynamic optical glass material establishes hierarchy by placing navigation and controls (tab bars, sheets, toolbars) on a distinct translucent layer floating *above* content rather than painting glass onto raw content cards. Crucially, the HIG requires automatic fallback to high-contrast solid surfaces under **Reduce Transparency**, disabling specular/parallax physics under **Reduce Motion**, and avoiding heavy custom fills that fight edge legibility.
- **Apple Right-to-Left (RTL) Guidelines** (`https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/right-to-left`):
  - **Mirror:** navigation bars, back/next chevrons, lists, side drawers, sliders, and progress indicators (`leading`/`trailing` constraints over `left`/`right`).
  - **Do NOT mirror:** clocks/timers, media playback controls (play/fast-forward), logos, or instruments/compasses (**Qibla compass**).
  - **Typography:** Never apply positive or negative `letter-spacing` to Arabic (breaks cursive ligature joins), align multi-line paragraphs to each script's own direction, and balance Arabic optical height slightly larger (~+2pt vs Latin caps) so English/Bangla/Arabic badges align gracefully.

### 2.2 Google Material Design: Material 3 Expressive & Bidirectionality (`2025–2026`)
- **Material 3 Expressive** (`https://m3.material.io/get-started/m3-expressive`): Backed by Google UX research across tens of thousands of users, M3 Expressive advocates **intentional visual hierarchy through contrasting typographic scale, springy responsive motion, and HCT semantic color roles** rather than rigid minimalism — helping users spot primary actions noticeably faster. **Critical caveat highlighted by Google's own guidance:** while younger users respond warmly to expressive dynamism, calmer/classic users prefer restraint; teams must tune expressiveness to context rather than maximizing visual noise.
- **Bidirectionality & RTL** (`https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/bidirectionality-rtl`, `https://m2.material.io/design/usability/bidirectionality.html`): Enforce CSS logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`, `inset-inline-start`), preserve raw digits/phone numbers/URLs in LTR, isolate embedded Arabic Ayahs inside Bangla/English paragraphs using explicit `<span dir="rtl" lang="ar">` or Unicode isolation (`unicode-bidi: isolate`) so punctuation never jumps.

### 2.3 Multi-Script Open Typography & Calm Habit Patterns
- **Font Licensing Verified (`SIL OFL v1.1` vs Restricted):**
  - **Quranic Arabic:** **`Amiri Quran`** (SIL Open Font License v1.1) provides authentic calligraphic Bulaq/Naskh shaping with generous Tashkeel clearances and unrestricted offline/commercial app bundling — avoiding the proprietary modification/redistribution restrictions found in **`KFGQPC Uthmanic Hafs`**.
  - **Arabic UI:** **`Noto Sans Arabic`** or **`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`** (`SIL OFL v1.1`), clean modern legibility cleanly paired at `+1.5–2pt` relative optical scaling without `letter-spacing`.
  - **Bangla UI & Body:** **`Hind Siliguri`** (`SIL OFL v1.1`, Indian Type Foundry, 820+ glyphs harmonized vertically with Latin baselines) paired with **`Noto Sans Bengali`** (`SIL OFL v1.1`). Typographic testing confirms Bangla's upper *i-kar* (`ি`), matra headline, and lower vowel/conjunct extensions (`ু`, `ৃ`, যুক্তাক্ষর) clip below `1.2` line-height and require **`1.55–1.65` line-height** and `0` letter-spacing.
- **Calm Habit Psychology (`2025–2026`):** Leading research confirms hard streak `0` resets induce "streak anxiety" and churn. Effective humane replacements combine **weekly consistency rings / 30–365 day subtle heatmaps**, **non-punitive grace/excused days** (travel, illness, menstruation), **zero-blame welcome-back phrasing**, and **time-of-day relevance**.
- **Offline Astronomical Math (`adhan-js`):** `batoulapps/adhan-js` (`https://github.com/batoulapps/adhan-js`, MIT License) implements Jean Meeus *Astronomical Algorithms* locally with zero network dependency — enabling exact offline Prayer Times, Asr school selection (Shafi'i vs Hanafi), high-latitude adjustments, and **Qibla bearing** from North.

### 2.4 Lasting Patterns vs. Passing Trends *(Design Inference)*

| Lasting Patterns (Build Into Core System) | Passing Trends / Anti-Patterns (Deliberately Reject) |
|---|---|
| **Logical Start/End layout architecture & true `dir="rtl"`** — structural accessibility forever. | Full-screen glassmorphism over dense worship text (hurts contrast, battery & low-end GPU). |
| **Time-adaptive single primary Home action** (current prayer window + quiet daily rhythm). | Duolingo/slot-machine guilt notifications, mystery loot-boxes, and XP grind inflation. |
| **Forgiving Grace / Pause modes & 30-day consistency maps** instead of punitive zero resets. | Value-bait multi-minute quiz funnels masking surprise subscription/ad traps. |

| **Explicit WCAG 2.2 AA contrast roles** (Light, Dark, true-black OLED Night). | Floating multi-tier redundant navbars crowding 79 links into a cramped drawer. |

## 3. Evaluation of Distinct Design Directions

We evaluate three foundational directions requested in the brief (**A, B, C**) plus one purpose-built synthesis (**D**) calibrated for Ibadah Quest's core demographic (ages 13–35 + families/beginners, low-end phones, Light/Dark/OLED Night, Bangla/English/Arabic):

### Direction A: "Serene Sanctuary"
- **Concept:** Wellbeing-inspired sanctuary (Calm / Pillars lineage). Generous whitespace, soft cream/sage/charcoal surfaces, subtle pill controls, subdued typographic hierarchy, minimal numeric XP badges hidden inside Profile/Progress.
- **Trade-offs:** Deeply peaceful for prayer, dhikr, and Quran reflection; zero visual overload; lightweight CSS. Risk: feels purely utilitarian — teenagers/early-20s beginners seeking gentle momentum may under-engage without clearer milestone structure.

### Direction B: "Guided Journey"
- **Concept:** Warm, step-by-step milestone path on Home (inspired by Duolingo's sequential unit clarity softened into Finch-like encouraging cards): daily morning-to-night trail, illustrated milestone chapters, progressive beginner guides.
- **Trade-offs:** Highest initial clarity for new Muslims and children learning Wudu/Salah routines. Risk: sequential path graphics consume mobile viewport space on 360px screens, complicate RTL path reversal + 200% font scaling, require heavier illustration assets, and risk feeling childish to 25–35 adults.

### Direction C: "Modern Heritage"
- **Concept:** Editorial typographic dignity meets restrained Islamic geometric craftsmanship (Alhambra/Mushaf proportions, warm sand/parchment/deep teal/brass-gold accents, hairline 8-point geometric dividers, calm ceremonial typography).
- **Trade-offs:** Unmistakable Islamic identity separating Ibadah Quest from generic Western habit trackers; elevates Quran/Hadith reading reverently. Risk: overused geometry or filigree frames quickly turn noisy on small screens, add SVG paint overhead, and fight dense Bangla/Arabic conjuncts if restraint slips.

### Direction D: "Noor & Rhythm" *(Recommended Hybrid — Serene Sanctuary Foundation × Restrained Modern Heritage Craft × Gentle Journey Milestones)*
- **Concept:** Combines **Direction A's** calm whitespace, 4.5:1+ crisp surface discipline, and forgiving consistency rings with **Direction C's** warm parchment/emerald-teal/soft-gold editorial dignity and subtle structural geometry (strictly <5% screen chrome, hairline dividers, zero busy patterns), plus **Direction B's** beginner-friendly *Time-of-Day Rhythm* (Fajr -> Dhuhr -> Asr -> Maghrib -> Isha + gentle Guided Path sheets) without infantilizing adults.
- **Adaptive Surfaces:** Light (*Warm Alabaster / Emerald Ink*), Dark (*Deep Slate Pine*), and Night (*Pure OLED `#070B0A` Obsidian with muted amber/teal — zero glare for Fajr/Tahajjud*), keeping controls cleanly distinct per Liquid Glass hierarchy principles without expensive backdrop-blur stacking on low-end GPUs.

## 4. Recommended Direction & Explicit Anti-Patterns

### Recommendation: Adopt Direction D — *"Noor & Rhythm"* (Serene Heritage)
1. **Meets Beginners First Without Patronizing Adults:** Opens into a serene **Time-of-Day Worship Rhythm** — current Prayer + Next Prayer countdown sit **immediately above the fold**, followed by quiet Daily Focus goals and a 1-tap Tasbih/Quran launcher, keeping beginner Learning Journeys and Tools cleanly organized in 4 focused top-level destinations (`Today`, `Worship & Quran`, `Learn`, `Profile`).
2. **Excels Across Bangla, English, and Arabic:** Uses pure semantic surfaces with generous line-height (`1.6` for Bangla conjuncts, `2.0` for Uthmani Quran ayahs with Harakat) and clean CSS logical layout rules so toggling `বাংলা` / `English` / `العربية` mirrors RTL natively without colliding chrome.
3. **Zero Cost on Low-End Devices:** Uses crisp vector iconography + CSS tokens rather than live Liquid Glass GPU shaders, keeping startup under `< 600 ms` after asset bundling cleanly inside Vite.

### What to Deliberately Avoid (Non-Negotiable Red Lines)
1. **Guilt-Driven Streaks & Shame Language:** No `"Streak at Risk!"`, no mid-day `S.cs` drops to `0`, no passive-aggressive notification nagging. Always support Pause/Grace mode (travel, illness, menstruation) and show 7-day / 30-day consistency heatmaps.
2. **Slot-Machine / Confetti Overload:** Remove random loot-box rewards (`Mystery Box`, RNG drop rolls in `surprise-rewards.js`), eliminate 50-node DOM confetti explosions + loud unmuted chimes, and purge net-negative XP shop packs and presumptuous titles (`Friend of Allah`, `Pure Soul`, `Shirk/Kufr/Nifaq` level ranks).
3. **Pattern Clutter & Fake Heritage Kitsch:** Never place ornate tiled backgrounds behind Arabic Quran text, Bangla body copy, or prayer buttons; keep geometric motifs strictly as subtle 1px structural dividers or empty-state watermarks (< 6% opacity).
4. **Dark Patterns & Notification Abuse:** Zero onboarding questionnaires ending in surprise locks, zero hidden ads/trackers, zero unsolicited CDN pings at launch, and clear user opt-in before Location or Notification permissions.
5. **Copying Competitors' Visual Identity:** Do not mimic Muslim Pro's glossy promotional banners, Duolingo's bright emerald/arcade owl cues, or Hallow's Catholic liturgical indigo/gold branding; own Ibadah Quest's distinct calm coastal-sage, warm parchment, and deep-obsidian night identity paired with a refreshed geometric Crescent-Mihrab app mark (to be proposed for approval before replacing the `IQ` icon).
